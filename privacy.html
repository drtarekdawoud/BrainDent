BrainDent Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

BrainDent is dental practice management software. This policy explains how information is handled when using the software and its optional Google Drive integration.

2. Local Data Storage

Clinic records are designed to be stored locally on the clinic's computer and infrastructure. The clinic is responsible for controlling access to its devices and stored information.

3. Google Drive Integration

If the clinic chooses to connect Google Drive, BrainDent may use Google Drive to upload and retrieve backup files at the clinic's request or according to its configured backup settings.

4. Google Account Permissions

Google authentication is used to authorize access to the selected Google Drive functionality. BrainDent requests only the permissions necessary for the enabled integration.

5. Data Security

BrainDent is designed to use security measures intended to protect locally stored data and backups. Users should maintain secure passwords and protect their computers and Google accounts.

6. Data Sharing

BrainDent does not sell personal information. Data handling depends on the features enabled and the clinic's configuration.
